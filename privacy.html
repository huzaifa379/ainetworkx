Privacy policy

NetX Downloader runs on your phone and keeps its work there. This page says exactly what that means, and names every case where something does leave.

Last updated 2 September 2026 · applies to NetX Downloader 1.0.7 for Android

The short version. There is no account and no sign-up needed to use the app. We operate no server that receives your downloads, your history or your identity, and the build has no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting library compiled into it. Three things do reach us, all of them listed below: a feedback message if you write one, your name and address if you fill in the one-time "Stay updated" invitation, and a handful of first-run signals that carry no personal data.

1. Who we are

NetX Downloader ("the app") is published and backed by AI Network X (together, the "developer", "we", "us"). The app is distributed as an APK from this website. Community: community.ainetworkx.com.

2. What the app does not collect

  • No account, phone number or advertising identifier is required or gathered to use the app.
  • No list of the links you paste, the pages you open, or the files you download is transmitted anywhere. That history is written to a database inside the app's own private storage on your device, and it is gone when you clear the list or uninstall the app.
  • No downloaded file is uploaded, copied or scanned by us. Files are written straight to your device's public media folders — DCIM/VidDownload for video, Music/VidDownload for audio.
  • The build published on this site contains no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK and no crash-reporting SDK.
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone or call-log access is requested, and none is declared in the app's manifest.

3. Data that leaves your device

3.1 Requests to the sites you ask for

When you share or paste a link, the app contacts that website directly from your phone to read what qualities are available and then to fetch the media. Those requests carry what any browser request carries — your IP address, a user-agent string, and any cookies your phone already holds for that site. They go to the platform you chose (for example YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook or X), not to us. Those platforms handle that data under their own privacy policies, which we do not control.

3.2 The one-time "Stay updated" invitation

On the very first launch the app offers to keep you posted about new versions. It is shown once, whichever button you press, and it is entirely optional.

  • Press Not now and nothing about you is sent. The app reports only that the invitation was declined.
  • Press Keep me updated and the name and e-mail address you typed are sent to us, together with your app version, your phone model and Android version, and the time.

That address is used to e-mail you about new releases and new tools, and for nothing else. It is not sold, rented, shared with an advertiser, or used to build a profile, and nothing about what you download is attached to it. To be removed, write to support@viddownload.app and name the address.

3.3 First-run signals

So that we can tell how many people get past the first screen and how many can actually save a file, the app reports a few plain counts: that the welcome screen was seen, that the invitation was shown, whether it was accepted or declined, and whether the permission needed to save files was granted. Each one carries only your app version, your phone model, your Android version and the time — no name, address or identifier, and nothing about what you download.

The Feedback screen transmits what you type, when you press send:

3.5 Who processes it

All three of the above are sent over HTTPS to the developer's own automation service, hosted in France, and from there into the developer's own mailbox. There is no third-party analytics or advertising recipient.

3.6 The download count on this website

This is about the website, not the app: when the Download button on this site is used, the server records one row so we know how many copies go out and on what kind of device. That row holds the time, your IP address, the approximate location the address maps to (country, region, city — never a street address or GPS position), your browser and its major version, your operating system and version, whether you are on a phone or a desktop, which page the click came from, the app version being served, and how many times the button was pressed. Presses from the same address on the same day are collapsed into that single row rather than counted again, so the figure reflects people rather than taps.

It is used for one purpose: counting and understanding downloads, and spotting abuse such as a script hammering the file. It is not sold, not shared with an advertiser, and not used to build a profile or to target you. There is no cookie, no advertising pixel and no third-party analytics script on any page of this site, and none of it is linked to anything you do inside the app — the app itself still reports nothing about what you download. If you want your row removed, write to support@viddownload.app from the same network and say roughly when you downloaded.

4. Sign-in sessions and cookies

The app includes a browser so you can reach content that is only visible to a signed-in account — a private Instagram reel you follow, for example. If you sign in there, the session cookie is stored by Android's own WebView cookie store, inside the app's private storage on your phone. The app reads it back only to attach it to requests for the site it belongs to.

Those credentials are never sent to us and never leave your device except back to the site that issued them. They are also kept out of Android's automatic cloud backup, so a stored session cannot be copied off the phone. Clearing the app's data, or signing out inside the in-app browser, removes it.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect anything from them. Because there is no account system, we hold no age data at all; if you believe a child has sent us a feedback message or signed up, write to us and we will delete it.

7. Advertising

The build published on this site displays no advertising and embeds no ad network. If that ever changes, this policy will be updated before the build that contains it is published, the ad partner will be named here, and the data that partner receives will be described. We will not add an advertising or tracking library silently.

8. Security

Data stays on your device, which removes most of the risk a server would create, and leaves the rest with your phone: anyone holding an unlocked handset can read the downloads folder. Anything the app sends to us travels over HTTPS. Downloads themselves follow whatever connection the source site provides.

9. Retention, and your rights

What we hold is small and easy to act on: feedback messages and sign-up addresses, kept for as long as they are useful for support and release announcements; the first-run signals, which are counts with no name attached; and the website download records described in 3.6, kept as a running total. To see, correct or delete what we hold, write to support@viddownload.app. Everything else is on your phone and under your control — uninstalling the app removes the app's own data, and any files it saved to your gallery stay yours to keep or delete.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it, and the version of the app it applies to is named. Material changes will also be noted on the download page.
